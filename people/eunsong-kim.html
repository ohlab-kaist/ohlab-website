Eunsong Kim, M.S.

Research Interests

B cell biology · Mucosal immunity · Tissue-resident memory B cells · Autoimmune diseases

About Me

My research focuses on B cell biology, with particular interests in mucosal immunity and tissue-resident memory B cells. I am interested in understanding the establishment, heterogeneity, and regulation of tissue-resident memory B cells within local tissue environments. I am also interested in characterizing the phenotypic and functional features of B cells that emerge during autoimmune diseases such as systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE).
