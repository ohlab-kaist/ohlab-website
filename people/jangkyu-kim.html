Research Interests

About Me

I am interested in tissue-resident immune cell populations and the immunology of asthma. My work aims to understand how B cells and tissue-resident memory B cells contribute to distinct inflammatory forms of asthma and to identify B-cell-intrinsic mechanisms involved in disease.
